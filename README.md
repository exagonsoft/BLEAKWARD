## 🔥 KEEP THE HEART ALIVE

The wasteland is quiet.

It needs to stay that way.

Deep beneath the ruins of **Orthea**, something is still listening.

You command **Ward Zero** — a fragile settlement built around a damaged industrial Heart. Harvest what remains. Raise walls. Bring the machines back online. Build towers. Push farther into the dead frontier.

Every step forward makes the Ward stronger.

Every generator adds heat.  
Every harvester adds noise.  
Every tower adds light.  
Every expansion tells **the Hollow** exactly where you are.

### **Grow too slowly and you won't survive.**
### **Grow too quickly and neither will your walls.**

# ⛏️ THE WASTELAND DECIDES WHERE YOU EXPAND

Resources aren't just numbers in a menu.

They're **places on the map**.

| | Resource | Why You Need It |
|:---:|---|---|
| 🪵 | **WOOD** | Construction & emergency repairs |
| 🪨 | **STONE** | Walls, fortifications & structural upgrades |
| ⚙️ | **IRON** | Towers, weapons & advanced industry |
| ✦ | **GOLD** | Rare upgrades & high-value technology |

Resources appear in procedural clusters across the wasteland.

The Wood beside your Heart is easy.

The Iron deposit beyond your walls?

That's where things become interesting.

You can reach it.

You can build there.

### **But can you defend the road back?**

# 🌒 WHEN NIGHT COMES

Preparation ends.

The wasteland moves.

The Hollow emerge from the darkness and pressure the settlement you've created.

They don't care how beautiful your base looks.

They care about:

**open routes · exposed harvesters · weak walls · isolated towers · damaged infrastructure**

When the attack ends, victory isn't a reset.

Walls remain damaged.

Resources were spent.

Production was interrupted.

And somewhere outside the perimeter is the resource deposit you still need.

So you repair.

You rebuild.

And eventually...

### **you open the gates again.**

# ⚙️ A WORLD THAT CHANGES WITH THE SEED

BLEAKWARD's environment is procedurally generated using deterministic world generation.

```text
WORLD SEED
   │
   ├── WOOD CLUSTERS
   ├── STONE DEPOSITS
   ├── IRON
   ├── GOLD
   ├── VEGETATION
   ├── WASTELAND DETAILS
   └── EXPANSION OPPORTUNITIES
```

Resource types follow different scarcity and clustering rules.

The objective isn't chaos.

### **Every map should feel designed — without being the same map twice.**

# ☠️ THE HOLLOW

Long before Ward Zero, Orthea discovered **Emberstone** beneath the earth.

They built an industrial civilization around it.

They drilled deeper.

Extracted more.

Built bigger.

Until something answered.

The **Choir** spread through buried infrastructure, mines, tunnels, and forgotten machinery.

What remained became the Hollow.

Humanity learned one rule:

### **Stay small. Stay quiet. Stay dark.**

Ward Zero is about to break all three.

# 🌅 THE DAWN ENGINE

Ward Zero is only the beginning.

Beyond the opening scenario lies the legacy of Orthea and the unfinished **Dawn Engine**.

The campaign is planned across four major chapters:

**I — LIGHT THE WARD**  
Restart the Heart. Survive the first nights.

**II — SCARS OF ORTHEA**  
Push into the industrial ruins and discover what destroyed them.

**III — THE DEEP SIGNAL**  
Recover the Dawn Engine components and descend toward the source of the Choir.

**IV — DAWN OR SILENCE**  
Decide what humanity should do with the power that destroyed it once already.

Ward Zero won't simply become larger.

### **It will become yours.**

A quiet refuge.

A fortified industrial machine.

A dangerous extraction city.

Or something the old world would recognize far too well.
